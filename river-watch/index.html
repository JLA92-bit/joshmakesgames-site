    How primed is the catchment

    The same rain can pass harmlessly or cause a flood. It depends on whether Lake Eppalock is full, how wet the ground already is, and how high the Murray is where the Campaspe meets it.

    Lake Eppalock

    When the lake is full, inflows pass straight over the spillway and on to Rochester.

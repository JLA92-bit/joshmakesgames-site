How the rivers connect

The Coliban and McIvor Creek fill Lake Eppalock. Axe, Mount Pleasant and Forest creeks join below the dam. The Campaspe meets the Murray at Echuca, just downstream of the Goulburn. Tanks show how full each dam is. Select any gauge for its detail.

Where Rochester's water comes from

Water passing Rochester is what Lake Eppalock released a day or two earlier, plus Axe and Mount Pleasant creeks, plus creeks with no gauge at all. Each part is read at the time its water would have left its gauge, using measured travel times.

Campaspe, Goulburn and Murray

Echuca: three rivers meet

When the Murray is high, the Campaspe cannot drain away, so water backs up towards Echuca. The gap between the two gauges shows how freely the Campaspe is draining. Goulburn and upper Murray peaks take days to arrive, which gives warning.

Eppalock, Coliban, Eildon, Hume, Dartmouth

Dams and weirs

Dams are run for water supply, not flood control. A dam with room to spare can soak up a flood. A full dam passes inflows straight downstream, which is what happened at Lake Eppalock in October 2022.

Ground and climate

The same rain does very different things on dry and wet ground. Before October 2022 the catchment was saturated after a third La Niña year. Ground wetness is compared with the same month in every year since 1970.
